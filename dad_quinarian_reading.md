## His Natal Sun — The Soul's Foundation

**Position:** 12.7° Cancer → **21st Quinary** (10°–14°59' Cancer)

### Angel: NELCHAEL
> *"He serves against calumniators, charms, and works to destroy the power of evil spirits. This angel rules over astronomy, mathematics, geography and all abstract sciences; he influences the wise and philosophers. The person born under this influence loves poetry and literature, and has a passion for study; he will distinguish himself in mathematics and geometry."*
>

**Psalm 31:14:** *"But I trusted in thee, O Lord: I said, Thou art my God."*

> *"The Twenty-first Spirit is Marax. He is a Great Earl and President. He appeareth like a great Bull with a Man's face. His office is to make Men very knowing in Astronomy, and all other Liberal Sciences; also he can give good Familiars, and wise, knowing the virtues of Herbs and Stones which be precious."*

**Attributes:** Astronomy, herbs, stones, arts and sciences.

> Your father was born under the sign of wisdom and learning — a soul drawn to knowledge, the sciences, and philosophy. Nelchael marks the seeker of truth, the mathematician of the cosmos.

## The Moment of Passing — July 13, 2026, 11:30 PM

### 1. ASCENDANT — 6.0° Capricorn → **56th Quinary** (5°–9°59' Capricorn)

*The helm of the soul's transition — the Angel of the Passing*

### Angel: POIEL
> *"He serves to obtain what one wants. This angel rules fame, fortune and philosophy. The person born under this influence will be held in esteem by all for his modesty, moderation and agreeable humor; he will only make his fortune by talents and his conduct."*
>
> *"For the fulfillment of one's request."*
>
> — Lazare Lenain, *The Science of the Kabbalah*

**Psalm 145:14:** *"The Lord upholdeth all that fall, and raiseth up all those that be bowed down."*

**Attributes:** Wishes, fame, fortune, modest, agreeable, talented.

### Demon: GREMORY (Gamori)
> *"The Fifty-sixth Spirit is Gremory, or Gamori. He is a Duke Strong and Powerful, and appeareth in the Form of a Beautiful Woman, with a Duchess's Crown tied about her waist, and riding on a Great Camel. His Office is to tell of all Things Past, Present, and to Come; and of Treasures Hid, and what they lie in; and to procure the Love of Women both Young and Old."*

**Attributes:** Divination, treasures, love.

> Poiel as the Angel of the Passing is profoundly significant — *the fulfillment of one's request.* The soul's journey on earth is complete; the petition is answered. Gremory, who tells of all things past, present, and to come, and who reveals hidden treasures, marks this as a moment of revelation — all that was hidden is now seen. The love of women echoes the love of family, of mother, of those who grieve.

### 2. SUN — 21.5° Cancer → **23rd Quinary** (20°–24°59' Cancer)

*The self, the will, the center of being — in the 7th house of transition*

### Angel: MELAHEL
> *"He serves against arms and to travel in safety. This angel rules water, all products of the earth and principally those plants necessary to the cure of illnesses. The person born under this influence is naturally hardy and capable of undertaking the most perilous expeditions; he distinguishes himself through honorable actions."*
>
> *"Against weapons and for safety in travel."*

**Psalm 121:8:** *"The Lord shall preserve thy going out and thy coming in from this time forth, and even for evermore."*

**Attributes:** Against weapons and perils, medicinal herbs, disease, safety, travels, honor.

### Demon: AIM
> *"The Twenty-third Spirit is Aim. He is a Great Strong Duke. He appeareth in the form of a very handsome Man in body, but with three Heads, the first like a Serpent, the second like a Man having two Stars on his Forehead, the third like a Calf. He rideth on a Viper, carrying a Firebrand in his Hand, wherewith he setteth cities, castles, and great Places, on fire. He maketh thee witty in all manner of ways, and giveth true answers unto private matters."*

**Attributes:** Arson, witty, secrets, discreet.

> The Sun's Psalm is a blessing for the journey — *going out from this world, coming into the next.* Melahel ensures safe passage. Aim, who gives true answers to private matters, speaks to the soul now knowing what was once mystery. The Sun in Cancer (his birth sign) at the time of passing completes a full circle — he returns to the cosmic home from which he came.

*The body, the emotions, the irrational soul — in the 1st house of a new beginning*

>

**Attributes:** Prosperity, warfare, delivers prisoners, active, brave, stoic.

### Demon: OSO (Osé, Voso)
> *"The Fifty-seventh Spirit is Oso, Ose, or Voso. He is a Great President, and appeareth like a Leopard at the first, but after a little time he putteth on the Shape of a Man. His Office is to make one cunning in the Liberal Sciences, and to give True Answers of Divine and Secret Things; also to change a Man into any Shape that the Exorcist pleaseth."*

**Attributes:** Arts and sciences, occultism, shape-shifting.

> Nemamiah *delivers the prisoner* — the soul released from the body. Oso's *shape-shifting* is the ultimate transformation from earthly form to spirit. The Moon in the 1st house at the passing means: this is not an ending, but a new beginning. A new birth into spirit.
