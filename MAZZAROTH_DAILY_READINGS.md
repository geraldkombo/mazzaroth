### House Scoring Technique

Each house is scored by:
1. **Natal planet presence** — planets natally in the house add baseline weight
2. **Transiting aspects** — every transit aspecting a natal planet in the house contributes (base score x orb precision x planet weight x house weight)
3. **House cusp activation** — transits aspecting the house cusp itself score even with no natal planets present (ensures ALL 13 houses always register)
4. **House weights** — angular houses (H1, H4, H7, H10) weighted 2.0; succedent (H2, H5, H8, H11) weighted 1.5; cadent (H3, H6, H9, H12) weighted 1.0; H13 weighted 1.0

Each transit-to-house pairing has a **strategy** and **actions** derived from the synthesis of:
- The transiting planet's archetype (e.g., Neptune = vision/illusion/spirituality)

- The natal planet being aspected (e.g., natal Neptune = personal vision/idealism)
